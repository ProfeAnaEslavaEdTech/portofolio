Independent Learning Experience Designer · sole proprietor / freelance consultant

I design learning experiences that people actually finish, because I believe learning must be both engaging and easy to measure.

For more than 20 years I have worked in language teaching and learning, finding ways to turn complex, highly technical topics into clear, structured courses that regular people can understand. Whether I work with universities, large companies or product teams, my goal is the same: turn specialised material into simple digital education that helps people do their jobs better.

As a native Spanish speaker with C1 English, I don't only speak the language — I design learning programmes for it. I regularly manage and deliver full corporate eLearning projects in English, aligned with the client's style guide and compliant with official accessibility guidelines for learners with disabilities.

What I bring to a team

  • Solid learning structures. I design with well-known frameworks — ADDIE, SAM and Agile — adding games, microlearning and realistic problem-solving scenarios to keep learners engaged from start to finish.
  • Decisions based on data. I embed SCORM and xAPI directly in the lessons, connecting LMS data with learning analytics so we improve courses from real student results, not guesswork.
  • Accessible to everyone. I build in web accessibility and Universal Design for Learning from the first sketch, not as a late compliance fix.
  • Smart human + AI teamwork. Human experts make every teaching decision; AI tools are used only to create materials faster, without sacrificing quality.

Selected projects

  • The Academic Writing Journey. My flagship — a six-level gamified academic-English course, rated 10/10 for inclusive design by the Universitat Politècnica de València (2026); students made zero navigation errors in final testing.
  • Current research. A doctoral proposal submitted to the UPV for 2026–2027 on a new mobile-learning design framework — how short language lessons can safely use AI tutors while teaching students to use AI responsibly.
