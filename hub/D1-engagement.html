How I take a client from "a vague need" to a signed training strategy

A real engagement, anonymised. I work as a freelance Learning Experience Designer (sole proprietor): I run the whole cycle myself — analysis, design, stakeholder consulting, vendor coordination and delivery. Understanding the client's true pain took several conversations — not one. This is how a D1 actually unfolds: a first meeting, a short discovery call, a no-charge needs analysis, and only then a written service proposal.

How I charge — discovery is free, pricing is custom

The first meeting, the needs analysis, and the initial "here's what you need" deliverable are not charged. Work begins to be billed only after the client accepts the written service proposal — so the client sees real value before committing. Price is set per project and depends on its scope, sector and complexity; there is no fixed rate.
